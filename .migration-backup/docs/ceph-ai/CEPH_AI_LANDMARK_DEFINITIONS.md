# Lateral Cephalometric Landmark Definitions

Definition set: `ADP-LM-LAT-v1`
Status: engineering baseline requiring formal orthodontist ratification before gold-standard annotation or model training

## Coordinate and contour convention

- Original image coordinates: `(0,0)` is top-left; X increases right and Y increases down.
- Canonical model orientation: a true lateral cephalogram with the patient facing right. A mirrored or uncertain image is flagged before inference; coordinates are never silently flipped.
- Coordinates are stored in original-image pixels. Millimetres require verified calibration.
- For bilateral structures, use the specified side/contour consistently. When contours are separable but the definition does not prescribe one side, the annotation guide must select the image-receptor-side contour and record the decision. Do not average two contours automatically.
- `core` means required by the current lateral readiness gate. `optional` points improve specific analyses and never block historic reports.

## Frozen definitions

| Code | Full name / type | Exact anatomical definition | Double-contour handling | Expected region / neighbors | Common errors | Gate | Dependent measurements |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `S` | Sella / bone | Geometric center of the radiographic outline of the sella turcica, not a point on its wall. | Use the best-defined receptor-side sella outline; if overlapping outlines prevent a defensible center, mark uncertain. | Central cranial base; near `N`, superior/posterior to `Ar`. | Placing on dorsum/tuberculum; centering the whole skull rather than the fossa. | core | SNA, SNB, ANB derivatives, SN planes, Jarabak/Bjork, S-line construction |
| `N` | Nasion / bone | Most anterior point of the frontonasal suture in the midsagittal profile. | Prefer the receptor-side cortical/suture contour; do not average separated soft and bony profiles. | Anterosuperior face; anterior to `S`, superior to `A`. | Soft-tissue nasion; nasal-bone tip; wrong suture. | core | SNA, SNB, facial plane/angle, facial heights, McNamara reference |
| `Or` | Orbitale / bone | Lowest point on the inferior orbital rim used for the Frankfort horizontal. | Use the receptor-side orbit (anatomical side closest to detector); record ambiguity when rims are inseparable. | Inferior orbital margin; anterior to `Po`, near midface. | Selecting infraorbital canal, zygoma, or the lower of two contours without side rule. | core | Frankfort plane, FMA, facial-axis/Downs measurements |
| `Po` | Porion / bone | Most superior point of the external auditory meatus used for the Frankfort horizontal. | Use the receptor-side meatus; machine ear rods are not anatomical porion unless protocol explicitly defines a separate point. | Posterior cranial base; posterior to `Or`, near `Ar/Co`. | Ear-rod center, mastoid, or internal auditory structure. | core | Frankfort plane, FMA, Downs/Ricketts references |
| `ANS` | Anterior nasal spine / bone | Most anterior tip of the bony anterior nasal spine. | Follow the sharper receptor-side maxillary contour; mark uncertain if spine is bifid/obscured. | Anterior hard palate; anterior to `PNS`, superior to `A`. | Soft-tissue columella, incisor alveolus, or nasal-floor midpoint. | core | Palatal plane, maxillary length/vertical references |
| `PNS` | Posterior nasal spine / bone | Most posterior tip of the hard palate/posterior nasal spine. | Use the posterior end of the receptor-side hard-palate contour; do not select pterygoid structures. | Posterior hard palate; posterior to `ANS`, near `Pt`. | Pterygomaxillary fissure, soft palate, or double-image midpoint. | core | Palatal plane, maxillary length, tracing contour |
| `A` | Point A (subspinale) / bone | Deepest point on the anterior maxillary alveolar concavity between ANS and the maxillary incisor alveolar crest. | Use the midsagittal anterior maxillary contour; if two cortices are visible, choose the contour continuous with ANS/incisor alveolus per protocol. | Anterior maxilla; between `ANS` and `U1A/U1T`; anterior to `S/N`. | Selecting incisor root, ANS, or an arbitrary midpoint rather than maximum concavity. | core | SNA, ANB, Wits AO, A-N perpendicular, maxillary diagnosis |
| `B` | Point B (supramentale) / bone | Deepest point on the anterior mandibular alveolar concavity between the lower incisor alveolus and Pogonion. | Use the midsagittal symphyseal contour; do not average inner/outer cortices. | Anterior mandible; between `L1A` and `Pog/Pm`. | Tooth root, `D`, `Pm`, or deepest lingual rather than labial concavity. | core | SNB, ANB, Wits BO, B-N perpendicular, mandibular diagnosis |
| `Pog` | Bony Pogonion / bone | Most anterior point of the bony mandibular symphysis in the midsagittal profile. | Use the external labial cortex; ignore soft-tissue chin and internal cortical duplication. | Anterior chin; below `B/Pm`, above/anterior to `Gn/Me`; posterior to `SPog`. | Soft-tissue Pogonion, mental protuberance transition, or root contour. | core | Facial plane/angle, Y-axis references, hard-tissue fallback for S/E lines |
| `Gn` | Gnathion / constructed bone | Point on the bony chin midway between the most anterior Pogonion and most inferior Menton, conventionally located by the bisector of facial and mandibular planes. | Construct from the selected midsagittal external chin contour; do not average right/left mandibular borders. | Inferoanterior chin; between `Pog` and `Me`. | Treating it as the lowest chin point or simple image-coordinate average without the defined construction. | core | GoGn-SN/MP-SN, facial axis, lower facial geometry |
| `Me` | Menton / bone | Most inferior point of the bony mandibular symphysis. | Use the external midsagittal symphyseal border; if inferior borders are doubled, apply receptor-side rule and flag uncertainty. | Lowest chin; inferior to `Pog/Gn`, anterior to mandibular body. | Soft-tissue menton, lower mandibular border away from symphysis, or `Gn`. | core | Mandibular plane, FMA, facial heights, Jarabak ratio |
| `Go` | Gonion / constructed bone | Posteroinferior mandibular angle point constructed at the bisector/tangent intersection of posterior ramus and inferior mandibular body borders. | Construct from one consistent receptor-side ramus/body contour; never combine opposite-side borders. | Mandibular angle; inferior to `Ar/Co`, posterior to `Me`. | Picking the visually lowest corner, antegonial notch, or mixing two mandibular contours. | core | Mandibular plane, FMA/IMPA, gonial/Jarabak angles, ramus/body tracing |
| `Co` | Condylion / bone | Most posterosuperior point on the mandibular condylar head according to the chosen side convention. | Current contract selects the condyle farther from the film when both are visible; this rule must be clinically ratified and kept consistent across annotations. | Superior posterior mandible; above `Ar/Go`. | External auditory structure, coronoid, or inconsistent side selection. | core | Effective mandibular/maxillary lengths and McNamara relationships |
| `Ar` | Articulare / constructed bone | Radiographic intersection of the posterior mandibular ramus/condylar contour with the inferior cranial-base contour. | Use contours from one consistent side; flag if no defensible intersection is visible. | Posterior cranial base; below/posterior `S`, above `Go`, near `Co`. | Anatomical joint center, basion, or arbitrary point between skull and ramus. | core | Saddle/articular/gonial angles, Bjork sum, Jarabak polygon |
| `D` | Point D / bone | Current Aqlan contract: point on the labial symphyseal contour approximately midway between B and the bony chin; exact construction must be ratified before annotation. | Use the same external midsagittal symphyseal contour as `B/Pm/Pog`. | Anterior symphysis; inferior to `B`, superior to `Pm/Pog`. | Confusion with geometric center of symphysis used by other analyses; arbitrary interpolation. | core | Current tracing/analysis compatibility; no new clinical use until ratified |
| `Pm` | Protuberance menti / bone | Point where the anterior symphyseal contour changes from concave to convex above Pogonion. | Use the external midsagittal chin contour; flag a broad/indistinct transition. | Between `B/D` and `Pog`. | Using Pogonion, Point B, or an interior symphyseal point. | core | Ricketts/chin morphology and tracing compatibility |
| `U1T` | Upper central incisor tip / tooth | Incisal edge tip of the most anterior maxillary central incisor selected for analysis. | Select one tooth consistently (usually the clearest/receptor-side or most protrusive per protocol); never average two incisors. | Anterior maxillary dentition; paired on the same long axis with `U1A`. | Lateral incisor, bracket, midpoint of overlapped tips, or mismatched apex. | core | U1-SN, U1-NA angle/distance, interincisal angle, occlusal plane, VTO |
| `U1A` | Upper central incisor apex / tooth | Root apex of the same maxillary central incisor represented by `U1T`. | Follow the same selected tooth contour; flag open apex, overlap, or root resorption. | Superior/posterior to `U1T` on approximately the same long axis. | Apex of the other incisor, canine, alveolar crest, or mismatched tip/apex pair. | core | U1-SN, U1-NA, interincisal angle, VTO |
| `L1T` | Lower central incisor tip / tooth | Incisal edge tip of the most anterior mandibular central incisor selected for analysis. | Select one tooth consistently; never average overlapped incisal edges. | Anterior mandibular dentition; paired with `L1A`. | Upper incisor tip, bracket, lateral incisor, or mismatched apex. | core | L1-NB angle/distance, IMPA, interincisal angle, occlusal plane, VTO |
| `L1A` | Lower central incisor apex / tooth | Root apex of the same mandibular central incisor represented by `L1T`. | Follow the same selected tooth; flag overlap, open apex, or resorption. | Inferior/posterior to `L1T` on approximately the same long axis. | Apex of another tooth, symphyseal cortex, or mismatched tip/apex pair. | core | L1-NB, IMPA, interincisal angle, VTO |
| `LS` | Labiale superius / soft tissue | Most anterior point on the vermilion border/profile of the upper lip. | Use the visible profile outline, not ghosted contralateral shadow. | Anterior soft-tissue profile; inferior to `Cm`, near upper incisor. | Subnasale, lip shadow, or bony/maxillary point. | core | Steiner S-line, Ricketts E-line, nasolabial angle, soft-tissue profile |
| `LI` | Labiale inferius / soft tissue | Most anterior point on the vermilion border/profile of the lower lip. | Use the primary soft-tissue profile and flag lip motion/poor exposure. | Anterior soft-tissue profile; inferior to `LS`, anterior to lower incisors. | Labial sulcus, soft Pogonion, or image artifact. | core | Steiner S-line, Ricketts E-line, soft-tissue profile |
| `Pn` | Pronasale / soft tissue | Most anterior point of the nasal tip on the soft-tissue profile. | Use the primary profile contour; ignore superimposed opposite nostril shadow. | Most anterior nose; superior/anterior to `Cm/Sn`. | Bony nasal tip, columella, or alar shadow. | core | Ricketts E-line, nasolabial angle, soft-tissue analysis |
| `Cm` | Columella point / soft tissue | Most anterior/inferior tangent point on the nasal columella used with Pn and LS for the nasolabial construction; protocol tool must display the exact tangent convention. | Use the primary soft profile; flag broad/unclear columella. | Between `Pn` and upper-lip/subnasal region. | Subnasale, upper lip, or nostril margin. | core | Nasolabial angle, Steiner S-line midpoint construction |
| `SPog` | Soft-tissue Pogonion / soft tissue | Most anterior point of the soft-tissue chin profile overlying bony Pogonion. | Use the external skin profile only; flag posture/object overlap. | Anterior to `Pog`, superior to soft-tissue Menton. | Bony Pogonion, labiomental sulcus, or soft-tissue Menton. | optional | Preferred endpoint for Steiner S-line and Ricketts E-line |
| `U6` | Upper first molar occlusal point / tooth | Mesiobuccal/mesial occlusal cusp point of the selected maxillary first permanent molar, according to the functional occlusal-plane convention. | Select the same side as the analyzed dental contour and do not average bilateral molars. | Posterior upper dentition; occludes near `L6`. | Second molar/premolar, distal cusp, bracket, or midpoint unrelated to occlusion. | optional | Functional occlusal plane and true Wits construction |
| `L6` | Lower first molar occlusal point / tooth | Mesiobuccal/mesial occlusal cusp point of the selected mandibular first permanent molar paired with `U6`. | Select the corresponding side/tooth and record uncertainty in mixed dentition or overlap. | Posterior lower dentition; near occlusion with `U6`. | Second molar/premolar, distal cusp, restoration, or mismatched side. | optional | Functional occlusal plane and true Wits construction |

## Anatomical plausibility checks

These checks create warnings and lower confidence; they do not silently relocate points:

- `ANS` must be anterior to `PNS` in canonical orientation.
- `Pog`, `Gn`, and `Me` must follow a plausible anteroinferior chin sequence on the same contour.
- `A` lies on the maxillary concavity between ANS and upper-incisor alveolus; `B` lies on the mandibular concavity between lower-incisor alveolus and Pogonion.
- `U1T/U1A` and `L1T/L1A` form plausible tooth axes and each pair belongs to one tooth.
- `U6/L6` lie in the first-molar/occlusal region.
- `Po-Or` yields a plausible Frankfort plane; outliers are reviewed, not auto-corrected.
- Soft-tissue points lie on the external profile, not within bone.
- Region, neighbor, visibility, and orientation failures produce explicit reason codes and mandatory review.

## Versioning rule

Changing a definition, side/double-contour convention, construction, alias mapping, or core/optional status creates a new landmark-definition version. Existing analyses and model evaluations remain pinned to their original version.
